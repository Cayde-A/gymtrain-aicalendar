# Vision and Goals

## Vision

TrainingOS is a personal training operating system that combines training plans, recovery data, PR tracking, calendar scheduling, and AI analysis into one repeatable feedback loop.

It is not just a workout calendar. The long-term goal is a system that knows:

- what was planned;
- what was actually completed;
- whether performance improved;
- whether recovery supports progression;
- what should change next time.

## North Star

Build a sustainable training system that helps reduce body weight toward 70-80 kg while preserving or improving strength and long-term athletic performance.

## Core Principles

- Performance first: PR and estimated 1RM are primary signals.
- Dynamic loading: weights change based on completion, RPE, and recovery.
- One source of truth: training, body, recovery, and nutrition data should eventually live in one database.
- Calendar as interface: Google Calendar reminds and structures training, but should not become the main database.
- Garmin as provider: Garmin contributes physiology data, but does not define training logic.
- AI as coach layer: AI interprets data and recommends progression, maintenance, or deload.

## Success Criteria

- A workout can be understood from the calendar without extra explanation.
- A completed workout can be logged in less than 60 seconds for the MVP.
- The system can identify new PRs across 1RM, 3RM, 5RM, 8RM, and estimated 1RM.
- The system can recommend Increase, Keep, Reduce, or Deload after a workout.
- Garmin recovery data can be attached to each training day.
- Future integrations can be added without redesigning the whole project.
